4. Top Viva Examination Questions & High-Scoring Answers

Q1: How do you handle garment damage or accidental stains?
Answer: We implement a dual-shield strategy: standard users have a pre-authorized security deposit held during the rental window. Incidental wear-and-tear insurance (₹99) covers minor seam pulls or washable stains. Irreparable damage is assessed during the return inspection in our admin portal against the retail replacement value.
Q2: Why use React 19 + TypeScript over a multi-page framework?
Answer: Fashion rental relies heavily on real-time client calculations: instant price recalculation when swapping durations (1 vs 3 days), dynamic date validation in the calendar, delivery speed toggles, and live rider simulation. React SPA gives seamless 60 FPS transitions without page reloads.
Q3: What are the unit economics and profitability of the rental model?
Answer: An authentic sherwani procured at ₹15,000 rents at ₹1,800/cycle. It achieves break-even within 8 to 9 rental cycles. With a typical garment lifecycle of 25–30 rentals, gross margins exceed 70% per item inclusive of dry cleaning and courier logistics.
Q4: How does the StylePass 0% deposit feature mitigate fraud risk?
Answer: StylePass members undergo identity and mobile OTP verification upon subscribing. Since members pay a recurring subscription (₹499 to ₹1,999/mo), user retention is higher and repeat behavior significantly reduces bad debt risk compared to anonymous one-time guests.
